Common questions

How do I make a game from my notes?

Log in, open your dashboard, choose a file (or paste text) and press “Create my game”. The first chapter opens while the rest is still being built.

Which files work?

PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT, Markdown, HTML and images up to 25 MB. Text-based files work best. Scanned PDFs and photos need an AI service that can read images and are slower.

Why does building take a few minutes?

An AI plans your chapters, writes lessons and questions, and then a second pass checks every question against your material. Busy AI services can make it slower.

The game is not about my file. What happened?

Games are built only from the text of your upload. If the file had little readable text (for example a scanned PDF), the result can be thin. Try a text-based version, or send us a message with the file type.

How does the game adapt to me?

ARCANA records every answer, how long it took and whether you used hints. It estimates your mastery of each topic and changes your difficulty, hearts, hints and extra practice. Your dashboard explains the current setup in plain words.

I forgot my password.

If you see “Forgot your password?” on the log-in form, use it. If not, send us a message below from the email you signed up with.

How do I download or delete my data?

Open your profile (your name at the top right). “Download my data” saves a copy. “Delete my account” removes everything permanently.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Open the site in your phone browser and choose “Add to Home screen” to install it like an app. Touch controls appear in landscape.

Controls on a keyboard

A and D or the arrow keys to move, W or Space to jump, Shift to sprint, E to interact, 1 to 4 to answer, M to mute.
