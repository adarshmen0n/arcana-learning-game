Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

ARCANA AI is created and operated by Adarsh Menon. This page explains what we store, why, who receives it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, display name, ranger choice, and your password stored only as a salted hash (we cannot read it). If you sign in with Google, we receive your verified email address and a Google account identifier.
  • Your uploads: the text we extract from files or notes you submit is used to generate your game. We store the generated game (chapters, lessons, questions) on your account. We do not save the original file to our database.
  • Learning data: your answers, whether they were right, how long you took, hints used, topic mastery, chapter progress and scores. This powers your adaptive difficulty, roadmap and coach.
  • Support messages you send us, with the email address you give.
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps you logged in, and your IP address held briefly in memory to limit abuse. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

How we use it

To run your account, build and adapt your games, show your progress and coaching, answer support requests, protect the service from abuse, and fix problems. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

Leaderboard

Other students can see your display name, level and XP on the global leaderboard. Nothing else (no email, no answers, no files) is shown. You can hide yourself at any time in your profile.

Who receives it

  • AI providers. To write your game we send text from your upload to AI services (currently Google Gemini, Groq and OpenRouter, which may route to the underlying model makers). To write your coaching plan we send numbers about your performance and topic names, never your name or email. Free tiers of AI services may keep or use submitted content under their own terms, so do not upload confidential material.
  • Hosting and database providers that store and serve the app (Render for hosting and a managed PostgreSQL database service for storage).
  • Wikipedia (and a web search service, if one is configured) receives the text of questions you ask Arc Search so it can look up current facts.
  • Google if you use Google sign-in, and Google Fonts for the typefaces on our pages.
  • An email service if password-reset or support emails are enabled.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie (arcana_session) to keep you signed in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • Access and portability: in your profile choose “Download my data” for a copy of everything linked to your account.
  • Deletion: in your profile choose “Delete my account” to remove your account, games, answers and progress. Support messages are kept without your account link so we can keep a record of help we gave.
  • Correction or other requests: use Help and support.

Retention

We keep your data until you delete your account. Sessions expire after 30 days. Password-reset links expire after 60 minutes.

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, connections use HTTPS, and every request is checked so you can only reach your own data. No online service is perfectly secure, so use a strong password that you do not use elsewhere.

Children

ARCANA is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
